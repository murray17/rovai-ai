# Taste Critic

Use this to judge proposed directions, `PROJECT_DESIGN.md`, and final screenshots.

## Scoring

Score each UI from 1 to 10 across:

- Product fit: Does the visual direction match the product's real job and audience?
- Hierarchy: Can the user see what matters first, second, and third?
- Clarity: Are labels, actions, data, and states easy to understand?
- Taste: Does it feel intentional without looking templated?
- Restraint: Did the design avoid unnecessary decoration, filler, and style cosplay?
- Density: Is the amount of information appropriate for the task?
- Craft: Are spacing, type, alignment, contrast, and component states polished?
- Responsiveness: Does the design survive mobile and narrow layouts?

## Red Flags

Treat these as serious failures:

- The redesign looks more complex but less useful.
- A reference style is visible but the product identity is weaker.
- Charts, labels, or controls overlap.
- Dark mode reduces readability without adding functional value.
- The page looks like a generic SaaS template.
- The design adds hero copy, cards, or stats that do not serve the user task.
- The strongest existing product cue was removed without reason.
- The UI passes build checks but fails visual judgment.

## Critique Questions

Ask before approving implementation:

- Is this better than the original UI?
- What exactly improved?
- What got worse?
- Would a user complete the task faster or with more confidence?
- Did the reference strengthen the product or merely recolor it?
- Which part should be simplified?
- Which part needs more craft?

## Compare Variants

When comparing variants, do not reward novelty alone.
Prefer the direction that best balances product fit, clarity, taste, and implementation realism.
