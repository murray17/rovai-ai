# Camp Companion Asset Notice

Source: `rovai-camp-companions-ui-decision-pack-v1.1`, reviewed 2026-07-27.

- `glyph-day.svg` was programmatically drawn from the character briefs.
- `bust.png` and the Day portrait PNG are generative-art first drafts.
- `preset.json` was normalized for v0.14: `handle` became `handleBase`, and `avatarRef` uses the
  controlled `rovai://member-avatar/...` namespace.
- Concept boards, landscape illustrations, raster glyph duplicates, HTML previews and generation
  process files were intentionally excluded.

These files are approved for implementation and packaged-App acceptance only. They are **not yet
approved for public release**. Public distribution remains blocked on character consistency,
transparent-edge, Day composition, small-size recognition, brand and
copyright review as required by `docs/versions/v0.14/architecture.md`.
